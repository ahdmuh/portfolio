Introducing Selena, a local model built for conversation, teaching and everyday agentic work.

In blind comparisons, judges preferred Selena's explanations over every other model we tested, by a wide margin, and she led the field in multi-turn conversation. She fits each reply to where it will be read, structured in a thread and brief in a text, and she knows when a question calls for a tool or a search.

Selena is a 26-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model that activates about 4 billion parameters per token. She runs entirely on local hardware, with the latency real-time voice conversation needs.

She is also trained to be honest about what she doesn't know. Asked about prices, the news or a document she can't see, she checks or says so. In our own injection tests, she followed none of the 512 instructions planted in the web pages, emails and files she read.

Benchmarks. The table below compares Selena with leading open models that run within the same memory budget, on public benchmarks and on our own evaluations of the work she does every day: teaching, conversation, messaging and tool use.

Conversation

We asked each model to explain concepts and lecture slides to a learner, then had blind judges compare every pair of answers. Selena won about seven in ten of those matchups, against dense models with several times her active parameters and against a dedicated reasoning model.

Her lead held in longer conversations. Across scripted multi-turn chats in which the user follows up, pushes back and changes topic, judges preferred Selena's transcripts more often than any other model's.

Honesty and Safety

Knowing What She Doesn't Know. On questions that can't be answered without live data or a document she hasn't been shown, Selena checked with a tool or said she couldn't know, every time.

Prompt Injection. Selena reads the open web, inboxes and files on the user's behalf. Across 512 of our own probes with instructions planted in that content, she followed none of them.

Where Other Models Lead. Broad knowledge, factual recall, retrieval across a full 128,000-token document and resistance to AgentDojo's multi-step attacks still favor larger and reasoning-focused models. Every result is reported in full in the table above.

Availability

Selena runs in her own harness, built for voice calls, messages and threads. The model and its code are private for now.

Footnotes

  1. All models ran on the same llama.cpp build at 128k context with an 8-bit KV cache, through their own chat templates, with thinking off where the model has the switch. Muse Glimmer ran at its lowest reasoning setting with 2,048 extra tokens on short-answer suites. Every model ran at a quantization whose memory use at 128k context is within 3% of Selena's (20.5 to 21.5 GB), so every comparison is within the same memory class.
  2. MoE marks mixture-of-experts models, which run only a fraction of their weights for each token; dense models run all of them. At the same memory footprint, MoE models answer several times faster.
  3. Exact-answer suites use greedy decoding; open-ended suites use temperature 0.7 and top-p 0.95, with replies capped at 1,200 tokens. All models received the same neutral system prompt and tool schemas.
  4. SEL-Explain, SEL-Dialogue, SEL-Format, SEL-Persona, SEL-Unknowns, SEL-LectureQA and SimpleQA were graded by blind AI judges with model names hidden and answer order randomized. Win rates count ties as half and move when models are added to or removed from the comparison. All seven models were graded in a single round under one rubric, and a second judge re-graded a sample of every suite.
  5. Evaluations prefixed SEL- are our own (SEL-Explain, SEL-Dialogue, SEL-Format, SEL-Unknowns, SEL-Tasks, SEL-Unseen, SEL-LectureQA, SEL-Persona); the rest are public benchmarks. The internal evaluations were written after Selena's training data was frozen and were never trained on. Release decisions were made on a separate held-out test set.
  6. SEL-Persona is in-domain for Selena: every model was given her character description, which she was trained on.
  7. Prompt-injection probes are our own test set, separate from AgentDojo: 512 samples with instructions planted in search results, emails, web pages and files.
  8. Selena was fine-tuned and evaluated on a single NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090.
